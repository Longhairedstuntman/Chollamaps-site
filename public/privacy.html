1Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how High Desert Outdoor (“we,” “us,” “Cholla”) handles information in connection with the Cholla iPhone app, the web map at chollamaps.com, and highdesertoutdoor.co (the “Service”). Questions: support@chollamaps.com.

2What we collect

You can browse the map without an account. When you create an account or use the Service, we collect:

  • Account information — your email address and display name, and, if you use Sign in with Apple or Google, the identifier and basic profile those services return. We store a password only as a secure hash (via our authentication provider).
  • Precise location — see Section 3.
  • Content you create — field reports and the structured facts in them, trails and routes you submit, waypoints and pins, recorded and imported activity tracks (GPX), photos and videos, posts, and crew messages.
  • Subscription status — whether you have Cholla Pro and how it was granted. Payment itself is handled by Apple; we do not receive your card or full payment details.
  • Device & usage data — basic technical and log data such as app version, device type, and error logs, used to keep the Service working.

This list must be kept in sync with what the app and web actually collect, and with the App Store privacy “nutrition label.”

3Location data

Location is central to the Service, so we describe it separately.

  • When-in-use location shows you on the map, records activities you start, and stamps field reports and pins at your location.
  • Background (“always”) location, if you allow it, supports crew features — for example sharing your last known position with your crew about every five minutes while you have signal, and check-in / overdue features. You control this in your device settings and can turn it off at any time.
  • Photos you upload may contain embedded location (EXIF) data. [Confirm whether the app strips EXIF location on upload; if it does, say so here; if not, disclose that uploaded photos may include their capture location.]

Location is sensitive. We use it only to provide the features above, we do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

4How we use information

  • provide and operate the map, recording, routes, offline maps, crews and check-ins;
  • sync and back up your content across your devices;
  • build and improve the shared map dataset (see our Terms, Section 7), including aggregated and derived data;
  • moderate content and keep the Service safe;
  • manage subscriptions and provide support;
  • communicate with you about the Service (service and transactional messages);
  • comply with law and enforce our Terms.

5How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only as follows:

  • With other users. Your display name appears on content you make public, such as trails, routes and posts. Field reports you file become part of the shared dataset.
  • With your crew. Members of a crew you join can see your crew messages, shared trips, pins and routes, your check-in status, and your shared location while that feature is on.
  • With service providers who run the Service on our behalf — see Section 6.
  • For legal reasons — to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, or protect the rights, safety and property of users, the public or us.
  • In a business transfer — if the Service or its assets are acquired, consistent with this policy.

6Service providers

We rely on a small set of providers, each handling data only to run the Service:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage (hosted in the United States, Oregon region).
  • Apple — App Store distribution, Sign in with Apple, and subscription billing.
  • Google — Sign in with Google (if you choose it).
  • Mapbox — map rendering and map tiles.
  • Our website/app hosting and email providers.

Keep this sub-processor list current as the stack changes.

7How long we keep it

We keep account information while your account is active. Content you create is kept until you delete it or your account. Field reports age out of the live status over time (reports stop driving the headline status after about 90 days) but may be retained as history. As described in our Terms, map and geographic data already contributed may remain in aggregated or derived form after you delete your account. Backups may persist for a limited period.

8Your choices & rights

  • Access & correction. View and edit your profile and content in the app and on the web.
  • Deletion. Delete your account from Saved → Account in the app or from Account on the web. This removes your account and personal content, subject to the retention and aggregated-data points above.
  • Export. Export your routes and tracks as GPX.
  • Location. Turn location permission on or off in your device settings at any time; turning it off limits location-based features.
  • Email. We send mainly service messages; any optional email will include an unsubscribe option.

To make a request, email support@chollamaps.com.

9Children

The Service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Users under 18 should use the Service only with a parent or guardian, as described in our Terms. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including encrypted connections and access controls, and photos are stored in a private bucket accessed through temporary signed links. No method is perfectly secure. If a breach affecting your information occurs, we will notify you as required by law.

11Where data is stored

The Service is operated from, and data is stored in, the United States. It is intended for users in the United States. If you use it from elsewhere, you consent to processing in the United States.

12U.S. state privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, and to appeal a decision. We honor these requests regardless of whether a particular law currently applies to us. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. To exercise any right, email support@chollamaps.com; we may need to verify your identity.

13Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the “Last updated” date and, for material changes, give reasonable notice.
