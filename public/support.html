Support

We’re a small team and read every message.

›Get in touch

Email us at support@chollamaps.com. We typically reply within a couple of business days. To help us help you faster, please include:

  • your device and app version (iPhone model and iOS, or your browser for the web map);
  • what you were doing and what happened;
  • the area or trail, if it’s about map data;
  • a screenshot, if you have one.

›Common things

Report wrong or missing map data

Tap a trail, road or spot on the map and use the report option, or email us the location and what you saw. Field reports are most useful when they’re filed at the location with a date.

Report content or a user

Use the report and block options in the app on any post, report or crew member, or email us.

Manage or cancel Cholla Pro

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Manage or cancel in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Refunds are handled by Apple. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Delete your account

Open Saved → Account in the app, or Account on the web, and choose to delete your account. See our Privacy Policy for what happens to your data.
